# Fully Funded Capital Stack v0.1 — Verification Record

Status: deterministic synthetic verification, 2026-08-30.

## Verified boundary

The implementation was compiled as strict C++20 with MSVC `/W4 /WX
/permissive-`. Debug and Release configurations are tested separately. The
engine accepts only a structurally validated untranched synthetic portfolio,
a declared success-participation term, and the strict capital-stack companion
file. Its probability envelope is either supplied directly as a declared
candidate input or generated by the candidate-only joint-cohort path from the
same hash-bound portfolio.

This record verifies arithmetic and software invariants. It does not validate
real project cash, probability calibration, enforceability, reserve custody,
fair value, market price, rating, tax, or regulatory treatment.

stated otherwise; percentages and years are labeled separately.

intermediate, and `[10,20]` senior tranches. Every scenario calls 0.2 of pool
cost pro rata: 0.04, 0.06, and 0.10. All projects draw fully at month zero.

## Cohort-to-waterfall integration

The thin bridge calls the joint-cohort engine first and passes its generated
primary envelope and the same portfolio object to the existing waterfall. The
bridge separately publishes the hash-bound candidate portfolio view and the
selected-`q` underlying view that actually enters the waterfall. The unit
regression proves equality with direct ambiguity and capital-stack calls, exact
scenario-bound identity, correct project receipts and NPV at a nontrivial fixed
`q=0.37`, invariance of draws and loss, and
`calibrated_execution_authorized=false`. Repeated cluster identifiers and a
declared reference outside the primary set return no stack; incompatible
participation or stack terms throw rather than falling back.

The dedicated CLI regression verifies both the successful and blocked modes,
the bound portfolio and ledger hashes, the disclosed non-hash-bound term-file
boundary, the selected-`q` project risk table, full shared-witness tail
attribution, and exit codes `0`, `1`, `2`, and `3`. A second command-line run at
`q=0.37` rejects reuse of the cohort's `q=1` project receipts. Normalized
cohort, portfolio, and ledger output is explicitly labeled a semantic rendering
that requires recomputed hashes before rebinding; strict participation and
stack term renderings remain reloadable inputs.

For the candidate-only cohort fixture, the senior tranche has expected loss
`0 / 0.20 / 5.062013` and impairment probability
`0% / 2% / 50.620127%`. Its central NPV is `0.68`, but its minimum expected NPV
is `-4.668214`. The declared underlying `q=1` misses its zero robust NPV target
by `11.948831`. These adverse results are intentional evidence that the bridge
does not convert an uncertain pool into a robust asset by applying priorities.

## Adversarial controls

The dedicated tests establish that:

- gaps, overlaps, incomplete partitions, duplicate IDs, non-finite amounts,
  unsupported versions, unknown keys, duplicate keys, and invalid residual
  placement fail closed;
- every mandatory reserve, par-funding, cost-call, cash-priority, unchanged-
  cash, and unchanged-loss assertion must be explicit;
- premium, discount, or fair-value claims are rejected;
- an already tranched base portfolio cannot be silently overridden;
- sub-unit tranche notionals and inputs exceeding the two-million combined
  scenario-tranche-month work bound are rejected before allocation;
- priority caps cannot create or consume more non-principal cash than exists;
- project principal and unused-reserve return are separately attributed, pro
  rata when both have equal priority in the same month;
- pool costs remain extra expenses rather than tranche principal;
- a staged draw produces positive prefunding drag at a positive hurdle;
- a continuing claim occupies the stack as outstanding exposure and never as
  realized loss; and
- WAL is absent when a measure feasible within the candidate set produces no material expected
  principal cash at the documented `1e-10`-million numerical tolerance.

The staged test commits 10, draws 4 at month 6, and leaves the claim
continuing. Six of reserve returns at month 12 and pays senior first. The
first-loss tranche holds 3 of unresolved exposure and senior holds 1. Realized
loss is zero. Nominal stack cash still equals the underlying draw-as-needed
cash, while the earlier subscription produces worse NPV.

A separate horizon test combines 2 of project-principal recovery with 6 of
unused reserve. The 7 senior principal distribution is attributed 1.75 to the
project and 5.25 to reserve; the junior distribution is attributed 0.25 and
0.75. Those ratios prove that source labels cannot change with processing
order.

## Residual limitations

Version 0.1 deliberately excludes investor capital-call default, reserve
provider default or yield, project-by-project or contractual-source tranche
attribution, replenishing
triggers, cash sweeps, coupons, scheduled amortization, premium or discount,
secondary trading, tranche optimization, liquidity facilities, external
guarantees, taxes, post-horizon recovery, market-consistent pricing, legal
bankruptcy remoteness, ratings, and regulatory capital. The synthetic all-
drawn-at-zero fixture does not exhibit prefunding drag; the separate staged
test verifies that mechanic.
