# Reference-Project Selection

**Decision date:** 27 August 2026  
**Evidence cut-off:** 27 August 2026  
**Selected case:** Believer Meats' Wilson, North Carolina facility  
**Classification:** public-evidence failed-scale-up, insolvency, and asset-recovery
reference; not underwritten and not financeable from public evidence

## Decision

Wilson remains the first reference case, but for a different reason than the
original selection. It is no longer described as merely pre-operational.
Public evidence now connects a named large-scale facility, construction,
product-specific regulatory work, an FSIS establishment history, cessation
reporting, creditor litigation, a state receivership lead, a foreign-insolvency
lead, and an evolving asset-sale process.

Selection means the case will be used to reconstruct:

- which risks were visible before capital was committed;
- which evidence could have supported staged rather than unconditional capital;
- how completion, liquidity, regulatory, demand, IP, and recovery risks
  interacted;
- which financial mechanism, if any, could have purchased decisive evidence or
  allocated a narrow risk without concealing subsidy; and
- what safeguards would have protected animals, workers, consumers, public
  funds, and capital providers.

It does not mean the facility is operating, bankable, technically validated,
creditworthy, available for purchase, or demonstrably displacing animal
agriculture.

## Entity boundary

The public record must not collapse distinct estates or authorities:

- **Future Meat Technologies Inc.**, historically doing business as Believer
  Meats, is the U.S. entity named in North Carolina records and federal
  litigation concerning the Wilson facility.
- **Future Meat Technologies Ltd.** is the foreign debtor named in the Chapter
  15 court record. Its foreign representative's filed motion asserts that it
  owns the U.S. entity; that assertion is not treated as a final title finding.
- The same filed motion asserts North Carolina receiver appointments and
  separates parent technology and cell-bank rights from the U.S. facility.
  Receiver authority, IP control, leased equipment, permits, licences, and
  secured collateral require their own primary records and transfer analysis.

The current operator, financing obligor, authorized controller, asset perimeter,
IP rights, governing law, and sale authority remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED` in the
machine dossier until primary records are retained and reviewed.

## Why this is the first case

The dated screen uses five criteria:

| Criterion | Wilson public record | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Named large facility and address | Yes | Asset-level questions can be framed |
| Historical investment and capacity claims | Yes | Forecast claims can be tested against outcomes |
| Product-specific regulator trail | Yes | Product, process, and facility boundaries can be separated |
| Physical construction evidence | Yes | Completion can be separated from commercial operation |
| Distress, control, and recovery trail | Yes | Financing design can be tested against actual failure modes |
| Controlled operating and financial data | No | No calibration, pricing, or bankability conclusion |

This does not prove Wilson is globally the “best” case. The candidate universe
has not been exhaustively searched or scored on retained evidence. UPSIDE Foods,
GOOD Meat, Mission Barns, and licensed Singapore facilities remain comparators
for narrowly scoped product, process, regulatory, commercial, or operating
questions. Cross-project evidence cannot be pooled without an independent
comparability and scale-transfer analysis.

## Principal research risks

- **Survivorship and hindsight bias:** insolvency makes failure visible but can
  make earlier uncertainty look more obvious than it was.
- **Entity-collapse error:** U.S. facility assets, foreign-parent IP, leases,
  permits, and licences may sit in different legal perimeters.
- **Allegation-as-fact error:** complaints and sale reporting are leads until
  primary orders, claims, and transaction records are retained.
- **Completion error:** a built facility, FDA consultation, or historical FSIS
  grant does not prove sustained qualified output or adequate liquidity.
- **Demand error:** collaboration, tastings, forecasts, and MOUs are not
  contracted cash flow.
- **Mission-claim error:** capacity does not prove financing additionality,
  sold substitution, or reduced animal suffering.

## Decision and contact rule

The case remains at public-research status until the executable
[evidence gate](REFERENCE_PROJECT_GATE.md) passes. Planned investment, capacity,
regulatory history, litigation, receivership, and sale descriptions stay
source-tagged and outside model calibration.

No data request should be sent merely to former company management. Before
outreach, authority must be established for the U.S. receiver or other
controller, the foreign representative or trustee, relevant secured creditors
and lessors, and any IP owner or licensee. The project has prepared a request;
it has not contacted those parties or represented that financing is available.
