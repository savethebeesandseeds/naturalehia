08 / From model to investable asset

What must become real.

The model is valuable because it can refuse weak terms. A live instrument requires evidence strong enough to replace every invented economic input.

Implemented

Mechanics we can reproduce

  • Cash and principal identities
  • Joint state evaluation
  • Loss, tail and tranche allocation
  • Provider exposure and credit stress
  • Zero reconciliation controls

  • Cash, recovery and draw amounts
  • Probability ranges and dependence
  • Investor and provider hurdles
  • Success participation rights
  • Protection terms and capacity
Unknown

Facts required before live use

  • Enforceable project cash rights
  • Authenticated milestone evidence
  • Empirical loss and recovery data
  • Investor demand and market hurdle
  • Provider authority and capacity

No rent to us.

The honest boundary

Packaging creates no cash.

The model separates project receipts, recoveries and external support. A tranche can move loss; a guarantee can transfer it. Neither can repair missing commercial value, and no payment is assigned to Naturalehia.
