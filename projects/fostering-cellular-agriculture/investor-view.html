The model is valuable because it can refuse weak terms. A live instrument requires evidence strong enough to replace every invented economic input.

The honest boundary

Packaging creates no cash.

The model separates project receipts, recoveries and external support. A tranche can move loss; a guarantee can transfer it. Neither can repair missing commercial value, and no payment is assigned to Naturalehia.
