# Believer Wilson Critical Gap Register

**As of:** 27 August 2026  
**Case:** failed scale-up, receivership, foreign-insolvency, and unresolved
asset disposition  
**Rule:** missing means unknown—never zero and never management's base case

The machine manifest contains one explicit gap record for each of the 57
compiled requirements. This register groups them by decision consequence.

## Priority definitions

- **P0 — authority and perimeter stop:** no controlled reference, contact,
  acquisition, restart, or financing scope is valid until resolved.
- **P1 — calibration stop:** no physical, construction, operating, cost, or
  recovery distribution can be responsibly calibrated.
- **P2 — structure stop:** no candidate terms may be presented as
  comparison-ready, enforceable, executable, or priced.
- **P3 — impact stop:** no financing-additionality, displacement, welfare, or
  animal-outcome figure may be reported.

## Conflicts that must be resolved, not averaged

1. The 2022 USD 123.35 million figure is an initial planned investment. A 2026
   court-filed motion states, based on information provided to the foreign
   trustee, that about USD 154 million was invested in the plant structure alone,
   excluding production equipment. The scopes and dates differ; both require
   reconciliation to source records.
2. A company release anticipated a Q2 2024 opening; a city report later called
   the site a production facility; current FSIS status is Inactive; public
   reporting describes cessation. None alone establishes the complete operating
   chronology.
3. FDA records several earlier runs and an intended North Carolina scale-up,
   but no complete Wilson run population or achieved qualified output is public.
4. FDA consultation and a historical FSIS grant do not establish current lawful
   operation; the current FSIS grant status is inactive.
5. The ADM relationship is exploratory and non-exclusive, not admitted offtake.
6. Court-filed assertions separate foreign-parent IP and cell-bank rights from
   the U.S. facility entity. Brand identity cannot substitute for title,
   licence, or transfer rights.
7. Secondary sale reporting says no qualifying bids remained as of 24 August
   2026. Current sale authority and disposition require primary filings.
8. Sponsor descriptions identify upstream animal inputs, but quantity,
   replacement frequency, observed sales, displacement, and welfare conversion
   are missing.

## Close-out rule

A gap closes only when the exact source population is:

1. indexed under a stable record and document version;
2. retained inside the controlled dossier;
3. matched to a lowercase SHA-256;
4. tied to an extract and exact project/process/entity scope;
5. reviewed under stated procedures by a named competent reviewer;
6. approved with conflicts cleared or managed;
7. reconciled to related physical, legal, and financial controls; and
8. accepted by the responsible technical, legal, regulatory, financial, credit,
   or impact owner.

An interview, slide, selected example, assertion, elapsed date, model output, or
unsigned proposal cannot close a source-record gap. Adverse history remains in
the manifest after resolution.
