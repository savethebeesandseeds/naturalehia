## Executive conclusion

Wilson is no longer treated as a generic pre-operational facility. The current
public record supports a more important research question: how a heavily built,
regulator-reviewed cellular-agriculture project reached cessation, receivership,
an inactive federal inspection grant, and an unresolved sale process—and what a
responsible financing design should have required before, during, and after
scale-up.

Public evidence still cannot establish achieved output, unit economics,
bankability, current sale authority, collateral value, or animal impact. Every
compiled gate therefore fails.

## Current-status evidence

### FDA scope is not Wilson operating evidence

FDA's [CCC 039 inventory](https://hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=039&set=AnimalCellCultureFoods)
and [24 July 2025 scientific memo](https://www.fda.gov/media/188065/download)
are strong sources for the agency consultation and the sponsor-described
process. They are not a commercial run ledger. The memo does not disclose the
complete run population, failed runs, exact vessel scale, output, yield, cycle
time, downtime, inputs, utility intensity, or cost.

FDA's [24 September 2025 supplement](https://www.fda.gov/media/189007/download)
records removal of a soy-derived enzyme from the process narrative. It shows why
process versions and regulatory crosswalks must be controlled; it does not prove
implementation at Wilson.

### A grant history is not sustained operation

The [FSIS establishment page](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/fsis-inspected-establishments/future-meat-technologies-inc)
identifies Future Meat Technologies Inc. d/b/a Believer Meats, P2816, 4939 Lamm
Road, grant date 29 October 2025, and current status Inactive. The
[FSIS directory](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/establishments/meat-poultry-and-egg-product-inspection-directory)
states that inactive establishments no longer have a grant of inspection and
was updated 24 August 2026.

This establishes current inactive status. It does not establish the inactivation
date or reason, approved labels, commercial release, sustained output, or a
reactivation path.

### The legal estates are not the brand

A [court-filed Chapter 15 motion](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73236089/2/future-meat-technologies-ltd/)
states that the Israeli parent owns the U.S. subsidiary; that the U.S. entity
was formed to construct and operate Wilson; that limited and general receivers
were appointed in North Carolina; and that the foreign parent controls
technology and cell-bank assets. Those are attributed party assertions.

A [federal case docket mirror](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/north-carolina/ncedce/5%3A2025cv00792/223551)
records a receivership notice. The [official CM/ECF federal order; PACER login may be required](https://ecf.nced.uscourts.gov/doc1/131010657783?caseid=223551)
stayed that case pending state proceedings and required 90-day status reports.

Official state [docket order 43, entered 27 April 2026](https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/orders-of-significance/gray-constr-inc-v-future-meat-techs-inc-2026-ncbc-order-43)
confirms the limited receivership entered 31 December 2025, its conversion to
a general receivership on 6 February 2026, and Kevin L. Sink as receiver. It
recites Gray's assertions of more than USD 35 million unpaid under the
design-build agreement and more than USD 36.4 million under the related note,
and Ameris's assertion of more than USD 13 million before interest and other
charges. These are asserted balances—not allowed claims, final priorities, or
recoveries.

Official state [docket order 63, entered 27 July 2026](https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/orders-of-significance/gray-constr-inc-v-future-meat-techs-inc-2026-ncbc-order-63)
says the receiver was liquidating Believer's assets. It records a USD 3.25
million pre-receivership settlement payable by Believer to CHRP: USD 2 million
was paid in November 2025 and USD 1.25 million was due 14 August 2026. It also
states that CHRP was then unsecured. The USD 2 million payment was not an
estate distribution or recovery. Final allowed claims, asset schedules, sale
proceeds, expenses, distributions, and creditor recoveries remain unknown.
Neither order is yet retained in the controlled machine package.

The [Wilson County parcel index](https://rod.wilson-co.com/External/LandRecords/protected/v4/SrchParcel.aspx?bAutoSearch=True&gp=2792762147000)
records 2024 deed entries to Future Meat Technologies Inc., an easement to the

index does not prove current unencumbered title, balance, priority, or release.

### Sale status is unresolved

[Secondary reporting dated 24 August 2026](https://agfundernews.com/breaking-upside-foods-drops-bid-for-believer-meats-us-cultivated-meat-facility)
says a USD 50 million stalking-horse agreement ended, no qualifying bids were
received, and the receiver was considering next steps. That report is indexed
as an adverse diligence lead, not an admitted sale order. The dossier does not
state that the facility was sold or that any named bidder remains the buyer.

## Financing lessons to test

1. **Construction completion is not commercial completion.** A physical plant,
   FDA consultation, and inspection-grant history can coexist with absent
   sustained output, demand, liquidity, or unit economics.
2. **Scale evidence must be purchased in stages.** Capital release should be
   linked to independent comparability, commissioning, consecutive qualified
   runs, cost and capacity tests, and safe stop/workout rights.
3. **Liquidity is a technical risk control.** Contamination, ramp delay,
   requalification, buyer acceptance, and working capital can exhaust cash even
   after equipment is installed.
4. **The collateral is not one object.** Land, building, leased equipment,
   permits, labels, process data, IP, cell banks, inventory, and contracts can
   sit in different estates and lose value if separated.
5. **Demand must be contractual and creditworthy.** Collaboration and an MOU do
   not produce minimum revenue, acceptance, payment security, or lender rights.
6. **Recovery terms belong in the original design.** Liens, priority, licence
   continuity, step-in, decommissioning, and sale mechanics cannot be postponed
   until distress.
7. **Mission outcomes are downstream of observed sales.** Capacity, investment,
   or regulatory progress cannot establish financing additionality,
   substitution, or reduced animal suffering.

## Animal-use boundary

Sponsor descriptions in the FDA file identify fertilized chicken eggs and
animal-derived reagents in early cell-line or bank stages, while stating that
animal-derived reagents are not used after the secondary master cell bank.
Quantities, replacement frequency, validation use, supplier-chain animal
inputs, finished-product cultivated share, and actual substitution are absent.

No animal-impact figure is admissible. The project must separately establish
the financing counterfactual, observed output and sales, buyer displacement,
leakage and rebound, animal-input baseline, welfare conversion, coproduct
allocation, uncertainty, and independent review.
