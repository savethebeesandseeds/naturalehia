# Real-Transaction Acquisition Ladder v0.1

**Observation date:** 1 September 2026

**Status:** public acquisition plan; no transaction-consistent hurdle identified

## Purpose

The pricing engine needs more than financing headlines. It needs a paid price,
the exact claim bought, every dated cash right, buyer costs, loss and recovery
cash paths, and a defensible bridge to the proposed cellular-agriculture claim.
This ladder names the first three packages worth acquiring because each answers
a different financial-engineering question.

All three packages now also contain canonical
`claim_ledger_v0_1` directories under the
[Project Claim Ledger standard](../docs/PROJECT_CLAIM_LEDGER_V0_1.md). They use

facts. Package validity therefore does not imply expected-return, hurdle, or
portfolio admission.

## Why these three belong together

They are not pooled observations and they are not assumed comparable. They are
three separate building blocks:

1. **Private risk capital:** a fixed contractual return plus a conversion
   option for a facility that is not yet generating revenue.
2. **Senior construction capital:** scheduled principal and floating interest,
   supported by public guarantees and financial covenants.
3. **Failure-aware development capital:** repayment is contractually scheduled,
   while case-specific remission may be available at the agency's discretion
   after verified technical failure; early research is not treated as ordinary
   bank credit.

That is close to the instrument family this project is building: research risk
is funded differently from construction risk, and neither is confused with a
diversified production claim. A later pool may hold standardized project
claims, but only after each project exposes capital calls, states, cash rights,
recovery, and shared factors through the common financial interface.

## What the second acquisition added

Solar Foods' First North company description reports a EUR 15 million Danske
Bank export-credit facility signed in May 2022 and fully drawn for Factory 01.
It reports EUR 16.3 million of principal including a capitalized EUR 1.3
million commission, interest of 2.27% plus three-month EURIBOR through 2025 and
4.03% plus three-month EURIBOR thereafter, and quarterly principal of EUR
679,166.66 from 30 September 2024. Finnvera and Denmark's EKF are named as
guarantors. Audited company-wide long-term-loan repayments exactly equal two
scheduled instalments in 2024 and four in 2025. The
[retained dossier](solar-foods-factory-01-facility-2022-2025/PUBLIC_TRANSACTION_DOSSIER.md)
keeps that strong reconciliation signal separate from a facility ledger and
documents why the margins still do not identify expected return.

## What the third acquisition added

Meatable's 24 September 2024 release reports a EUR 7.6 million Innovation
Credit award and an intended use of improving productivity and reducing costs
before commercialization. The [retained dossier](meatable-dutch-innovation-credit-2024/PUBLIC_TRANSACTION_DOSSIER.md)
keeps that issuer observation separate from the official 2024 programme
opening and RVO's standard July 2024 obligations. The generic 3% base rate and
15% technical or 25% clinical fixed premiums are not assigned to Meatable
without its individualized decision and formal classification. Standard
reporting, repayment and pledge templates define diligence states, not award-
specific rights. Current RVO guidance confirms the key state boundary:
remission can be requested and may be granted depending on the circumstances;
technical failure does not automatically cancel principal. The missing
decision, advances, cash rights, security and outcome leave the candidate
ineligible for expected-return or hurdle calibration.

## Acquisition rule

The next package is admitted only if retained documents close a real economic
gap. More press releases do not increase the observation count. If a package
still lacks full cash rights, settlement, buyer costs, expected loss and
recovery, or a complete comparison bridge, it remains a visible rejected
record. The project will not replace the missing evidence with a coupon, an
issuer valuation, a policy rate, or a synthetic assumption.
