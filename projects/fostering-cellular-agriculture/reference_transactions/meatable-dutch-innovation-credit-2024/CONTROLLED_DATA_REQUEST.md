# Meatable Innovation Credit - Controlled Data Request

**Purpose:** obtain only the records required to convert the 24 September 2024
public award observation into an exact claim-and-cash reconstruction and to
decide whether it can enter a market-hurdle evidence set. This is not a general
management diligence request.

## 1. Exact recipient, decision and project

- Complete individualized award decision and every annex, project plan,
  project budget, milestone schedule, reporting schedule, amendment, increase,
  consent, waiver, suspension and finalization decision.
- Exact legal name and registration number of the recipient, each related
  liable party, pledgor and surety, and a reconciliation from the Meatable
  brand name used in the announcement.
- Award-application, decision, acceptance and effectiveness dates, with the
  formal technical-or-clinical classification and the legal version of every
  programme condition incorporated into the decision.
- Reconcile the announced EUR 7.6 million to the maximum approved credit,
  eligible project costs, aid percentage, later adjustments, final subsidy
  amount and any cancelled or undrawn amount.

## 2. Advances, settlement and proceeds

- Complete advance request and approval ledger by reporting period, including
  approved budget, milestone status, invoice or cost basis, amount, value date,
  payer, recipient bank account and any withheld or reversed amount.
- Redacted RVO disbursement notices, bank statements and general-ledger entries
  sufficient to reconcile committed amount, cumulative advances, outstanding
  principal, accrued interest, fixed premium and cash on every reporting date.
- Sources-and-uses ledger linking each advance to eligible project expenditure,
  with treatment of asset-sale proceeds, rebates, tax, capitalized items and
  expenditure funded from other sources.
- Recipient and provider legal, accounting, diligence, registration and other
  direct costs, with payer and payment date. Unknown costs must not be entered
  as zero.

## 3. Every dated cash right

- Exact base-rate, fixed-premium and other compensation clauses applicable to
  this award, including classification, advance-by-advance accrual basis, day
  count, capitalization, payment dates, default rate and tax treatment.
- Individualized repayment schedule and every revision, including forecast
  basis, principal, interest and premium by date, prepayment, acceleration,
  standstill, cure, default and final maturity.
- Creditor and debtor cash ledgers through the observation cut-off, reconciled
  to bank evidence. Scheduled cash is kept separate from settled cash.

## 4. Milestone, control and compliance history

- Signed progress reports, milestone certificates, accepted-cost schedules,
  RVO review results and the evidence supporting each achieved, delayed,
  revised, failed or unresolved milestone.
- Every request and decision concerning material budget, schedule, financing,
  commercial-prospect, control, financial-condition, business or IP changes.
- Required financial statements, forecasts, realization reports, asset lists,
  restriction tests, notices of breach and every cure, reservation or waiver.
- Final report, accountant declaration, subsidy-finalization calculation and
  final decision, or positive evidence that these events have not yet occurred.

## 5. Security, priority and recovery

- Executed pledge and every annex, annual asset list, registration and
  perfection record, showing the exact pledgor, first-ranking assets, future
  assets, IP, excluded assets, jurisdictions and effective dates.
- Existing and permitted liens, intercreditor arrangements, release and
  substitution rights, valuation reports, maintenance obligations and
  enforcement waterfall.
- Any executed surety or guarantee, including obligor, cap, exclusions,
  priority, recourse, demand requirements and settlement lag. A template or
  optional annex does not establish that a surety exists.
- Default, enforcement, asset-sale, workout and recovery records with dated
  gross proceeds, costs, distributions and residual obligations.
- Following the 19 December 2025 corporate announcement: executed board and
  shareholder resolutions, statutory dissolution or liquidation filings,
  liquidator appointment, effective dates, creditor register, admitted and
  disputed claims, liquidation accounts and every interim or final
  distribution.
- RVO's proof of claim or positive evidence that none was filed, including its
  legal basis, principal, interest, premium, priority, secured assets,
  enforcement or release, allowed amount, distributions, residual balance and
  closure status. Keep those records separate from Agronomics' equity
  investment and accounting write-off.

## 6. Failure and remission state

- Every technical-failure or market-prospect assessment, including who made it,
  the evidence set, scope, date and whether the finding was partial or complete.
- Any request for temporary or definitive remission, the exact amount and
  obligations requested, RVO's decision, conditions, effective date, security
  treatment and appeal or review status.
- Reconciliation of credit, interest, premium, paid cash, remitted amount,
  enforced recovery and residual claim immediately before and after each
  decision.
- Positive confirmation when no remission request or decision exists. Do not
  translate project failure into automatic forgiveness.
- Reconcile the board-and-shareholder resolution to dissolve and wind down the
  business against the credit state. Corporate dissolution, operating
  cessation and an equity write-off are not substitutes for a default notice,
  remission decision, proof of claim, collateral recovery or provider cash.

## 7. Ex-ante expected-cash reconstruction

- Exact observation timestamp used for valuation, no later than evidenced
  execution and settlement, plus the complete information set then available.
- Mutually exclusive success, delay, partial-failure, failure, remission,
  default and recovery states, with every dated advance and receipt.
- Independently reviewed physical probabilities and recovery method that do
  not use the observed price, policy rate, target price or reverse-engineered
  risk-neutral probability; retain sources, estimation method and uncertainty.
- Solver output, all roots or root failure, annualization convention and
  price-reconstruction residual. A generic programme rate is not an expected-
  cash preimage.

## 8. Universe, de-duplication and comparison bridge

- Frozen search universe, lookback, inclusion rule and de-duplication manifest
  covering the original decision, any increases, amendments, advances,
  refinancing and related public support. State the exact economic-cluster
  rule.
- For each comparison axis - contractual rights; priority and residual recovery
  risk; systematic, covariance, concentration and model risk; maturity;
  currency and hedge cash; liquidity; size; and observation date or regime -
  provide separately sourced signed lower and upper adjustments.
- Provide the exact jointly feasible adjustment set and connected projection;
  a componentwise box or hull is not eligible.
- Reconcile technical-loss and remission effects already placed in cash against
  residual rate adjustments so the same public risk absorption is not counted
  twice.

## Acceptance rule

Provide an indexed release with immutable file identifiers, document versions,
lowercase SHA-256 hashes, named reviewer, review date, procedures, conflicts
and open exceptions. Dutch originals control; translations must identify the
translator and version. Missing evidence stays missing, and later realized
outcomes remain separately labelled backtests. The project will generate a
`hurdle-evidence.cfg` only if a frozen, de-duplicated package produces an
eligible, independently reconstructed expected-cash rate preimage and an exact
comparison bridge. Neither the EUR 7.6 million award amount nor a generic
programme rate can substitute for that result. A permitted historical RNS
delivery or redistribution authorization is also required before the full
19 December 2025 announcement bytes can enter a public controlled release.
