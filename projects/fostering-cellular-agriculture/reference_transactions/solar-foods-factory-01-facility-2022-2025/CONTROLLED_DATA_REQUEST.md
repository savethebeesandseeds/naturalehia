# Solar Foods Factory 01 Facility — Controlled Data Request

**Purpose:** obtain only the records required to reconstruct the all-in lender
cash for the May 2022 Danske Bank facility and decide whether it can enter a
market-hurdle evidence set. This is not a general management diligence request.

## 1. Exact claim, draw, and fee cash

- Fully executed loan agreement, schedules, amendments, waivers, side letters,
  export-credit conditions, and every document defining the borrower and
  lender claims.
- Dated draw notices, bank settlement records, sources-and-uses ledger, and
  reconciliation of EUR 15 million borrower funding to Factory 01 expenditure.
- Complete EUR 1.3 million commission calculation showing whether it was paid,
  withheld, financed, or capitalized; recipient, payer, value date, and tax.
- Lender, arranger, legal, diligence, agency, hedging, guarantee, and other
  direct transaction costs, with payer and payment date.

## 2. Every dated loan cash right

- Full principal schedule, final maturity, final rounding payment, voluntary
  and mandatory prepayment, make-whole, cancellation, acceleration, and
  refinancing provisions.
- Three-month EURIBOR fixing source, observation and reset dates, tenor fallback,
  floor, day count, interest payment dates, compounding, default rate,
  withholding, tax gross-up, and margin-change rules.
- Facility-level ledger through the observation cut-off for draws, principal,
  interest, fees, prepayments, amendments, and any non-cash settlement.

## 3. Guarantee, security, priority, and recovery

- Executed Finnvera and EKF guarantees, coverage shares and caps, beneficiary,
  premiums, eligibility, exclusions, waiting period, claim procedure, payment
  timing, currency, subrogation, recourse, termination, and amendment history.
- Collateral schedule, lien and perfection evidence, negative-pledge scope,
  priority, permitted debt, intercreditor terms, and enforcement control.
- Default, workout, enforcement-cost, collateral-value, guarantee-claim, and
  dated recovery paths, including realized claims or recoveries if any.

## 4. Covenants and historical settlement control

- Definitions and calculations for minimum liquidity, gearing, environmental
  and social undertakings, test dates, cure rights, reporting, and consequences.
- Test certificates, advance consents, waivers, reservations of rights, and
  amendments for every period, including the 2023 breaches.
- Creditor-level reconciliation of the audited 2024 and 2025 principal and
  finance-cost lines to the Danske facility and all other borrowings.

## 5. Expected-cash and comparison reconstruction

- Frozen states for scheduled payment, prepayment, covenant breach, default,
  guarantee claim, restructuring, enforcement, and recovery, with every dated
  lender call and receipt.
- Exact execution, draw, settlement, and price-observation timestamp. Freeze
  physical probabilities to information available no later than that May 2022
  timestamp; keep all 2023–2025 covenant, consent, and repayment outcomes as a
  separately labelled ex-post backtest.
- Independently reviewed physical probabilities, provider-default treatment,
  and recovery method independent of the observed price, promised margin,
  target price, and any reverse-engineered risk-neutral probability; retain
  source lineage, solver method, roots or root failure, and reconstruction
  residual.
- Frozen search universe, lookback, inclusion rule, and de-duplication manifest
  that treats later audited records as performance evidence for the same
  facility rather than additional transaction observations.
- For claim rights; priority and residual recovery risk; systematic,
  covariance, concentration, and model risk; maturity; currency and hedge cash;
  liquidity; size; and May 2022-to-target regime, provide separately sourced
  signed lower and upper adjustments.
- Provide the joint feasible adjustment set and prove the displayed total is
  its exact connected projection; a componentwise box or hull is not eligible.
  Reconcile expected loss and guarantee recovery already placed in cash against
  residual risk-premium adjustments so neither is counted twice.

## Acceptance rule

Provide an indexed release with immutable identifiers, document versions,
lowercase SHA-256 hashes, named reviewer, review date, procedures, conflicts,
and open exceptions. Missing fields stay missing; a blank adjustment is not
zero. Later realized repayment data must remain a separately labelled
backtest. The project will generate a `hurdle-evidence.cfg` only if a frozen,
de-duplicated package produces an eligible, independently reconstructed
expected-cash rate preimage and an exact comparison bridge. A floating margin,
aggregate finance-cost line, or assumed guarantee recovery cannot substitute
for that result.
