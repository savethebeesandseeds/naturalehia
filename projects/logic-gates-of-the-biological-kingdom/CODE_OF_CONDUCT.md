# Community Standards

Logic Gates of the Biological Kingdom is intended to help people do careful
work together across disciplines. We welcome participants regardless of
background, identity, career stage, institution, or geography.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence without demeaning people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Give credit for ideas, code, data, experiments, and negative results.
- Make room for questions from people entering an unfamiliar field.
- Protect private, sensitive, and safety-relevant information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate misrepresentation, plagiarism,
and pressure to conceal inconvenient evidence are not acceptable.

These standards apply in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
meetings, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Concerns may be reported privately to
[contact@waajacu.com](mailto:contact@waajacu.com). Reports will be considered
in context and handled with as much confidentiality as practical. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or take other
proportionate action to protect the community and the integrity of the work.
