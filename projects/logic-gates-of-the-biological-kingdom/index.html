What exists today

Tested mathematical-model software, not a working protein.

No functional protein gate or sequence has been produced or experimentally validated by this project. The software now bounds predictions from an explicit two-state equilibrium mechanism while keeping those limits visible.

The purpose

Leave evidence people can trust.

The project is designed for future contributors as much as for its first result. Assumptions, failed candidates, uncertainty, provenance, and acceptance criteria belong beside the code—not behind a claim made after the fact.

Start with XOR

XOR requires the joint-input state to reverse the effect of either input alone, making non-additive molecular coupling the central scientific problem.

Build in the open

Linux, modern C++, local execution, open-source dependencies, deterministic tests, and an MIT license provide a foundation others can inspect and extend.

Name the evidence

A specification, simulation, candidate, measured state, and validated gate are different things. Project language preserves those differences.

The current milestone

A framework for predeclaring a continuous-model test.

The dependency-free library derives an active-state probability from ON and OFF binding partitions, locates model-analytic extrema, and reports missing criteria as not assessed. Its coupling audit is a paired mathematical ablation—not mechanism selection, folding, sequence design, kinetics, or biological validation.

Why we are doing this

Technical ambition with human responsibility.

The work is intended to support peaceful, beneficial research. Biological capability, safety, and information risk must be reviewed before scope expands.
